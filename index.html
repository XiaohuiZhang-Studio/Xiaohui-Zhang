Xiaohui Zhang

Landscape Architecture / Urban Research / Mapping

Profile

Xiaohui Zhang is a PhD researcher in Landscape Architecture, Planning and Society at the University of Copenhagen. Her research explores informal public performance spaces, migration, mapping, and everyday spatial negotiations in contemporary China. Through fieldwork, visual archives, and spatial storytelling, she investigates how marginalised groups reshape urban public life.
